# DECISIONS

Format: date — decision — why — alternatives rejected.

- 2026-11-10 — **2D formulation: conjugate cross-section unit cell, fully developed flow** — keeps all four design variables and tests exactly what the 1D model approximates (f·Re(α), Nu(α), fin efficiency, base conduction); exact analytical validation exists — rejected: streamwise parallel-plate plane (fin height drops out as a variable).
- 2026-11-10 — **Toolchain: gmsh (geometry + mesh) + scikit-fem (solve), pip-native via uv, Python 3.12** — installs natively on the M3 Pro with no compilers or Docker, fully scriptable, small enough to read, validated to 0.007% on f·Re and Nu_H1 (four aspect ratios, 1.6 s total) — rejected: OpenFOAM (needs Docker or a case-sensitive volume on macOS; overkill for a 2D linear problem), FEniCSx (conda-only, heavier), SU2/Elmer (not installed, compile or conda needed).
- 2026-11-10 — **3D path** — keep `make_geometry`/`mesh`/`run_cfd` as separate tools with file-based hand-off so a 3D solver (OpenFOAM or FEniCSx) can replace `run_cfd` later; gmsh already does 3D — not built in V1.
- 2026-11-10 — **1D screen lives in this repo, not thermal-mcp-server** — the server uses circular-tube constants (Nu = 4.36, f = 64/Re) with no fin efficiency; straight-fin model is added here first and upstreamed after the demo.
- 2026-11-10 — **1D Nu: Shah & London four-wall Nu_H1 polynomial + fin efficiency** — standard engineering practice and the constants are certain; the three-heated-wall table would be more faithful to the adiabatic cover and is a candidate upgrade once checked against the book — rejected for now: three-wall table from memory.
- 2026-11-10 — **1D stays fully developed (no entrance or minor-loss terms)** — parity plot should compare like with like; entrance effects are a stated limitation of both.
- 2026-11-10 — **Requirements: 1 kW uniform over 60×60 mm, PG25 at 40 °C inlet, ΔP ≤ 30 kPa core-only, copper k = 390, 2 mm base** — Riccardo's call.
- 2026-11-10 — **Manufacturing limits: ≥ 0.15 mm features, aspect ratios ≤ 10, fin height ≤ 4 mm (skived copper)** — Riccardo accepted defaults.
- 2026-11-10 — **Objectives: R_th vs ΔP (hero plot); pumping power reported only** — Riccardo's call.
- 2026-11-10 — **R_th = (avg base T − T_inlet)/Q, incl. base + caloric, excl. TIM and R_jc** — isolates what the plate design controls.
- 2026-11-10 — **License MIT** — matches thermal-mcp-server.
- 2026-11-10 — **Model use: Fable as main model, no separate advisor** — Riccardo's call; revisit at each usage check (delegate coding to Opus subagents if Fable usage passes ~50% before the Monday reset).
- 2026-11-10 — **PG25 properties from CoolProp `INCOMP::MPG[0.26]` (Melinder data) at 40 °C** — reproducible in code and citable; cross-checked against DOWFROST LC 25 typical data (μ = 1.15 mPa·s and k = 0.485 W/m·K at 50 °C vs CoolProp 1.18 and 0.487 at 47 °C) — rejected: hand-typed datasheet values; note inhibited commercial fluids run about 1–2% denser and Dow lists a higher cp (4.13 vs 3.98 kJ/kg·K), not resolved.
- 2026-11-10 — **SPEC.md frozen** — Riccardo approved.
- 2026-11-10 — **Base thickness stays 2 mm, as a labelled assumption** — published designs span 0.1 mm (manifold microcooler) to 4.5 mm (commercial 55 mm plate) with no typical value; 2 mm sits mid-range, adds a fixed 1.4 mK/W and does not change design ranking — rejected: 1 mm (no stronger evidence for it).
- 2026-11-10 — **thermal-mcp-server is not a dependency of V1** — its physics (circular-tube constants, no fin efficiency, no PG25) does not apply and importing it unused would be decoration; the straight-fin model here is the upstream candidate — rejected: adding it for its resistance-network structure alone.
- 2026-11-10 — **Recommendation rule: knee of the CFD-verified front (farthest from the end-to-end chord in log-log space); lowest-R_th design within budget reported alongside** — deterministic and explainable; open to Riccardo overriding with a temperature target.
- 2026-11-10 — **Sweep is full factorial, 8 × 6 × 7 × 3 = 1008 designs; shortlist is 10 points evenly spaced in log ΔP along the 1D front** — simple and reproducible — rejected: LHS (no benefit at 3 ms per sweep).
- 2026-11-10 — **Level 2 scope approved as a follow-on: 2D planar Navier-Stokes; sine-wave fins excluded** — prototype showed no new flow structure in 2D up to Re 200; the published benefit is from 3D Dean vortices — candidate showcase: slot-jet impingement.
- 2026-11-10 — **Assumptions live in ASSUMPTIONS.md (one row each, with status and basis); AGENTS.md is the cold-start entry point** — makes implicit choices auditable and lets another agent or model resume without chat history — rejected: leaving them spread across SPEC, DECISIONS and the report.
- 2026-11-10 — **Merge policy: branch per milestone, pull request into main, merge when CI passes** — keeps main runnable and gives a readable history for the public repo — rejected: committing straight to main.
- 2026-11-10 — **Level 2 showcase: confined slot-jet arrays with interleaved return slots, flat base, fluid-only energy with uniform wall flux** — the flow physics is in-plane so 2D is honest, and the unit cell is periodic — rejected: wavy fins (3D effect), conjugate base in the first pass (adds plumbing, small effect on the average).
- 2026-11-10 — **Level 2 lives in `level2/`, separate from the frozen V1 pipeline in `tools/` and `run_study.py`** — keeps the approved spec untouched; integration into one study runner is a later decision.
- 2026-11-10 — **Rated flow 1.5 LPM (1.5 LPM per kW) and channel-core ΔP budget 6 kPa at rated flow; flow is no longer a design variable** (SPEC Amendment 1) — Riccardo: 30 kPa was too generous for the core alone; a budget only means something at a stated flow; 1.0 and 2.0 LPM are reported as sensitivity on the recommended design; the grid is refined to 12 × 8 × 7 = 672 designs at one flow — rejected: 30 kPa core budget, flow as a fourth design variable.
- 2026-11-10 — **Thermal-resistance target R_th ≤ 15 mK/W** — Riccardo: 55 °C average chip interface at 1 kW and 40 °C inlet — rejected: no target (left the recommendation to a geometric rule).
- 2026-11-10 — **Recommendation rule: lowest ΔP among CFD-verified designs meeting both budget and target; else lowest R_th within budget, stated as a fallback; the knee is reported as secondary only** — replaces the knee as primary because the knee depends on where the front's end points are, which the design-space bounds set — rejected: knee as primary.
- 2026-11-10 — **Slot-jet minimum gap and slot width 0.1 mm; Level 2 sweep at rated flow over slots 0.1/0.2 mm, gaps 0.1/0.15/0.2 mm, pitches 1/2/4 mm (18 cases)** — Riccardo: 50 µm laser-cut features exist but only at low volume; jets and fins now compared at equal flow — rejected: 0.05 mm gaps.
- 2026-11-10 — **Plots show the region of interest only: ΔP from 0.3 kPa to 1.5× budget, R_th from 0.7× the lowest feasible value to 2× target, with budget and target lines and the shaded window that meets both** — nobody designs outside it, and the full range hid the decision — rejected: autoscaled axes over the whole sweep.
- 2026-11-10 — **Model use: Fable orchestrates and reviews; Opus subagents implement; Haiku subagents do web surveys** — Riccardo's call at 22% Fable usage — each delegated task gets a written brief with acceptance criteria and is reviewed before merge.
- 2026-11-11 — **Plan-view Level 2 for fin planforms (SPEC Amendment 2): sinusoidal, zigzag, offset strips, circular and diamond pins, with straight fins computed the same way** — Riccardo asked for more ambitious topologies against the Amendment 1 requirements; the plan view captures the in-plane flow these planforms create — rejected: 3D (no budget), cross-section models (cannot represent interrupted fins).
- 2026-11-11 — **Developed-periodic values from a streamwise-periodic cell (Patankar, Liu & Sparrow 1977), not from the last periods of a developing strip** — at Pr 11.5 wavy channels need about 10 periods (20 mm) to settle, which a developing strip per design cannot afford in the budget; the periodic cell gives the developed value exactly and a developing strip on one design per topology checks it (all within 1% of the periodic value by the last period) — rejected: developing strip for every design (minutes each, and an undeveloped last period would overstate h).
- 2026-11-11 — **Tall-fin network: floor gets the side-wall h; adiabatic-tip fin efficiency from the plan-section P_c and A_c; continuum lateral layout** — same structure as Level 1, so the plan view is checked against Level 1 CFD on straight fins (ΔP −7.1%, R_th −5.7%) — rejected: an end-wall correction (no validated basis yet).
- 2026-11-11 — **Wavy channels keep a constant normal gap w with the lateral pitch set so the thinnest fin is t; zigzag corners rounded to a 0.05 mm inner radius; offset strips classic (g = 0, rows not overlapping laterally); the 0.15 mm gap limit is the true minimum distance between solids** — the simplest constructions that respect the brief's limits — rejected: sharp zigzags (singular offset walls), laterally overlapping strips.
- 2026-11-11 — **Level 1's H/w ≤ 10 is not applied to plan-view designs (only the Amendment 2 limits), and is flagged in the notes of designs that break it** — the brief lists H/t_min ≤ 10 only — open for Riccardo.
- 2026-11-11 — **Cross-topology recommendation by `tools.pareto.recommend` over all converged plan-view designs** — same rule as Level 1 — the pick (circular pins, 14.90 mK/W at 0.80 kPa) is a plan-view number and inherits the tall-fin bias; see PROGRESS open questions.
- 2026-11-11 — **Two-tier thermal requirement: threshold R_th ≤ 20 mK/W (must meet), objective R_th ≤ 15 mK/W (want)** (SPEC Amendment 3) — Riccardo: the threshold is what a plate alone must beat against the OCP 0.02 °C/W case-to-inlet figure; the objective leaves about 5 mK/W for the interface material — rejected: a single 15 mK/W target (made every recommendation a 0.5% margin call).
- 2026-11-11 — **Channel-core ΔP budget 5 kPa at rated flow, one third of a 15 kPa whole-plate allocation** — consistent with an estimated 4.6 kPa core for the Ortega et al. skived plate scaled to 1.5 LPM; still an allocation, not a sourced limit — rejected: 6 kPa (no stated basis).
- 2026-11-11 — **Recommendation by marginal return with cut-off 0.25 (`tools.pareto.recommend_marginal_return`), replacing "lowest ΔP meeting the target" and the knee** — walks the eligible CFD front from its lowest-ΔP point and keeps moving while some further point returns e = −ln(R_j/R_i)/ln(ΔP_j/ΔP_i) ≥ 0.25; says what the extra pressure buys rather than stopping at a hard line; `recommend` and `knee_index` stay in `tools/pareto.py` with their tests but are no longer used in any report — rejected: lowest ΔP meeting the target (knife-edge at the target), the knee (set by where the front's end points are).
- 2026-11-11 — **Capacity at the threshold rise, capacity per kPa and kW per pumping W are reported for every CFD-verified design but not used to choose** — both ratios always favour the lowest ΔP inside the window, so they cannot select a design on their own.
- 2026-11-11 — **Level 1's H / gap ≤ 10 applied to plan-view results, with gap = the true minimum fluid gap** (SPEC Amendment 3, item 6) — the same skiving limit as Level 1; excluded designs stay in results.json, listed with the reason; offset strips, circular pins and diamond pins fell below 5 eligible designs, so 5, 4 and 6 designs were added (`--extend` solves only new designs) — rejected: re-solving the whole sweep.
